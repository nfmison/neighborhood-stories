The Great Hanshin Flood of 1938

Drag the divider upward or downward to compare the original photograph with the modern reconstruction.

↕
1938 photograph Modern reconstruction

This photograph was taken after the Great Hanshin Flood of July 1938. It shows the Sumiyoshi River in what is now Higashinada Ward, Kobe. The large traditional building overlooking the river is the Hakutsuru Fine Art Museum.

Torrential rain over the Rokko Mountains transformed the river into a violent torrent carrying rocks, mud and debris downstream. The modern reconstruction presents the historical scene as it might have appeared if photographed today.
